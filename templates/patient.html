Stance, swing, cadence, stride time and variability are measured directly from the heel-strike and toe-off events in the shank gyroscope. Stride length and walking speed are modelled from the shank excursion and depend on this patient's stride-length calibration on the device — check it before reading anything into an unexpected value.
